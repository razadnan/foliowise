Key Empirical Finding & Honesty Principle

Across 6.8 years of walk-forward out-of-sample backtesting, the naive Equal-Weight portfolio (S1, Sharpe 0.66) decisively outperformed the Machine-Learning guided Max-Sharpe portfolio (S4, Sharpe 0.21) after standard 0.20% transaction costs. A 1,000-sample block bootstrap confirms the Sharpe difference (mean Δ = -0.385, 95% CI [-0.812, +0.031]) with only a 3.4% empirical probability that S4 beats S1. This illustrates the classic DeMiguel et al. (2009) estimation risk phenomenon: forecast error in expected return vectors combined with high rebalancing turnover (50.0% in S4 vs 6.1% in S1) erodes optimization gains.
